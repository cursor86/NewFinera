ACCOUNTANT SALARY AUSTRALIA 2025–26

NewFinera Editorial Team · Updated August 2026 · General education only

Accounting is one of the few professions in Australia with two competing — and equally recognised — qualification pathways: Chartered Accountants ANZ (CA) and CPA Australia. Which one an accountant holds matters less to their salary than which type of firm they work for, since Big 4 and top-tier firms consistently pay above mid-tier and suburban practices at every level.

SALARY RANGE BY CAREER STAGE

Graduate accountants typically start in the $60,000–$70,000 range, with Big 4 firms usually sitting at the upper end. Once CA- or CPA-qualified with 3-5 years' experience, $80,000–$100,000 is common. Senior accountants and Financial Controllers often move into the $110,000–$150,000 band, and CFOs at larger organisations can exceed $200,000 — a career-long spread of well over 3x from graduate to the top of the ladder.

WHAT ACTUALLY MOVES ACCOUNTANT PAY

  • Qualification pathway: CA and CPA are the two dominant routes, both requiring supervised experience alongside further study — the choice matters less for pay than the employer sponsoring it
  • Firm tier: Big 4 firms (Deloitte, EY, KPMG, PwC) generally pay more at graduate level than mid-tier or small local practices, though mid-tier firms often offer a faster partnership track
  • Public practice vs industry: moving in-house to a commercial/industry role after 3-5 years often brings a pay uplift compared to staying in public practice
  • Registered tax agent status: becoming registered with the Tax Practitioners Board opens up higher-paying senior and principal roles that involve signing off client returns
  • Tax season loading: public practice accountants often work substantial extra hours during the July–October tax season, which isn't always reflected in base salary alone

CAREER PROGRESSION

A common path runs from Graduate Accountant through qualified CA/CPA, then Senior Accountant, Financial Controller, and potentially Chief Financial Officer. Many accountants also specialise into tax, audit, or advisory, each with a distinct trajectory — audit tends to have the clearest partnership track at public practice firms, while tax and advisory specialists often move fluidly between public practice and industry.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Is CA or CPA better for accounting salaries in Australia?

Both are respected qualifications; CA is often favoured by larger firms and audit-focused careers, while CPA is common across a broader range of commercial and public practice roles. Salary outcomes depend more on employer, specialisation, and experience than which qualification is chosen.

Do accountants need to be a registered tax agent to prepare tax returns?

To charge a fee for preparing and lodging tax returns for clients, an accountant generally needs to be registered with the Tax Practitioners Board (TPB), which requires specific qualifications and supervised experience.

How much more do senior accountants earn compared to graduates?

The gap is substantial — graduate accountants typically start well below six figures, while Financial Controllers and senior managers with 8-10+ years of experience frequently earn well over double a graduate starting salary, before considering CFO-level roles.
