ACCA-Qualified · Big 4 Experience

NewFinera is run by a team of ACCA-qualified accountants with Big 4 audit and financial compliance experience across manufacturing, retail, and service industries. We started NewFinera to bring that same level of financial rigor directly to small and medium businesses that would otherwise never have access to it — without the Big 4 overhead.

OUR MISSION

NewFinera exists to solve two related problems. First: small and medium businesses need accurate books and real financial visibility to make good decisions, but can rarely justify the cost of a Big 4 firm or a full-time finance hire. Second: individuals researching their own tax and money questions are often stuck with information that's scattered, jurisdiction-specific, or locked behind paywalls.

We address the first through our bookkeeping and Growth Advisory services for SMEs. We address the second through the free tax calculators and educational guides we've maintained covering Australia, the United Kingdom, the United States, Pakistan, and the UAE.

HOW WE KEEP OUR FREE CONTENT ACCURATE

Tax rates and rules change regularly — often annually through government budget announcements (Australia's Federal Budget, the UK's Spring/Autumn Budget, US IRS inflation adjustments, and Pakistan's Finance Act). We review and update our free calculators and guides to reflect current published rates, and we clearly date all content so you know how current it is.

OUR EDUCATIONAL PARTNERS

Our free content references and links to respected, independent financial education resources including Investopedia, Coinbase Learn, Binance Academy, BabyPips, Morningstar, and FX Street — connecting readers to authoritative information beyond what we cover directly.
